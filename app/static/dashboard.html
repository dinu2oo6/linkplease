LinkPlease · comment → DM

A durable order book of people we owe a message to, worked by one clerk who is only allowed to send 10 DMs per minute. Every number below is a COUNT(*) over rows in the database — nothing is counted in memory.
1 · RECEIVE
Write it down, then say OK
Signature checked, event stored, 200 returned in milliseconds. No matching or sending on this path — so 500 events in 10s can't make us drop any.
2 · DECIDE
Who is owed a DM?
Keyword match creates one obligation per (rule, person). It's a primary key, so the same person can't be owed twice — no matter how many times they comment.
3 · SEND
One clerk, legal pace
A slot is reserved in the database before every send, so the rate limit holds even with two copies of the app running.
4 · CONFIRM
Chase it until delivered
"Accepted" isn't "delivered" — about 15% fail afterwards. We poll every one and resend the ones that failed.
